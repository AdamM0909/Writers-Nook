    How the tags work
    
A file called Night Verses - Adam {Poems} [Poetry, Dark] (Night Cycle, Book 2).pdf shows up as Night Verses by Adam, on the Poems shelf, tagged Poetry and Dark, as book 2 of Night Cycle. Books named like Self-Appreciation 1, Self-Appreciation 2 are grouped into a series automatically. Tags are all optional.

  


  
To remove or replace a book, open the books folder on GitHub and delete the file.

  Published books are public to anyone with the link.
